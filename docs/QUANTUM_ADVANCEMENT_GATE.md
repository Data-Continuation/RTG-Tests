## Required conditions before Experiment 003 may claim a quantum result

All conditions must be satisfied before any claim stronger than reinterpretation is permitted.

1. The dataset and analysis plan must be identified before outcome inspection.
2. RTG observables must be mapped to independently measured quantities rather than assigned from the desired result.
3. At least one standard quantum-information null model must be implemented from the same data.
4. RTG must make a preregistered out-of-sample prediction that differs from the null model.
5. Preparation, classical communication, channel loss, postselection, synchronization, and detector effects must be included.
6. The prediction must survive blinded or held-out evaluation.
7. No-cloning, conservation, and no-signaling constraints must remain explicit.
8. Independent replication is required before a physical validation label.
